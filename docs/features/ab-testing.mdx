# A/B Testing

OutreachAI supports A/B subject line testing on marketing campaigns. Send two versions to a subset of your list and let the platform pick the winner automatically.

## How it works

1. Create a marketing campaign as normal
2. Enable the **A/B test** toggle
3. Enter a **Subject B** (your variant — Subject A is the original)
4. Set the **split percentage** (e.g., 20% = 10% gets A, 10% gets B, 80% gets the winner later)
5. Choose the **winning metric**: open rate or click rate
6. Set the **test duration** in hours (e.g., 4 hours)

When you send the campaign, OutreachAI splits the list and sends both variants. After the test window closes, it automatically sends the winning variant to the remaining recipients.

## Reading results

The campaign detail page shows side-by-side stats:

| Metric | Subject A | Subject B |
|---|---|---|
| Sent | 500 | 500 |
| Opens | 120 (24%) | 165 (33%) |
| Clicks | 40 (8%) | 62 (12.4%) |

The winning variant is highlighted and labeled **Winner**.

## Tips

- Test one variable at a time (subject line only — keep body identical)
- Use at least 200 recipients per variant for statistically meaningful results
- 4–8 hours is a good test window for most lists
