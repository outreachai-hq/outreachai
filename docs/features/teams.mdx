# Teams and Workspaces

OutreachAI supports multi-user workspaces so teams can collaborate on campaigns, leads, and analytics.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a shared environment. All campaigns, lead lists, and settings inside a workspace are visible to all members (subject to their role). You can be a member of multiple workspaces and switch between them from the sidebar.

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access — can delete the workspace, manage billing, and change any member's role |
| **Admin** | Can invite/remove members, manage all campaigns and settings |
| **Member** | Can create and manage campaigns and leads |
| **Viewer** | Read-only — can view campaigns and analytics but cannot make changes |

## Inviting teammates

1. Go to **Settings** > **Team**
2. Enter the teammate's email address and select their role
3. Click **Invite** — they receive an email with a join link
4. The invite link expires in 7 days

## Switching workspaces

Click your workspace name in the sidebar footer to see a list of workspaces you belong to. Click any workspace to switch to it. Your active workspace determines which campaigns and data you see.

## Personal mode

If you haven't created or joined a workspace, you operate in personal mode. All your data is still private to your account.
