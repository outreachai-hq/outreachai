# Marketing Mailer

The Marketing Mailer is OutreachAI's broadcast email tool — equivalent to Mailchimp newsletters, but free and self-hosted.

## How it works

Unlike campaign sequences (which send one-to-one), marketing emails go to your entire lead list at once. Use it for newsletters, product announcements, or promotional offers.

## HTML editor

The built-in rich text editor supports full HTML. Paste your own HTML template or use the drag-and-drop composer. Preview renders the email exactly as recipients will see it.

## Open and click tracking

Every sent email includes a hidden tracking pixel and link-wrapped URLs. The campaign dashboard shows:

- Open rate
- Click rate
- Unsubscribes
- Bounces

## Unsubscribe compliance

OutreachAI automatically adds an unsubscribe footer to every marketing email. When a recipient clicks unsubscribe, their email is added to your unsubscribe list and excluded from all future sends.

## A/B testing

See [A/B Testing](/features/ab-testing) for how to test subject lines and pick a winner automatically.

## Scheduling

You can send immediately or schedule a marketing campaign for a specific date and time. Scheduled sends respect your account timezone.
