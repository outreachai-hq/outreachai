# Email Warmup

Email warmup gradually increases your sending volume over time so inbox providers trust your domain before you start cold outreach at scale.

## Why warmup matters

New sending domains and email accounts have no reputation. Sending 200 emails on day one triggers spam filters. Warmup establishes a positive sending history by exchanging real emails with other warmup participants.

## How the ramp schedule works

OutreachAI uses a progressive ramp:

| Day range | Emails per day |
|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | 3 |
| Days 8–14 | 8 |
| Days 15+ | 15 |

Warmup emails are real messages exchanged between enrolled accounts. Replies are sent automatically to simulate genuine engagement, which improves inbox placement.

## Enrolling an account

1. Go to **Warmup** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add account** and select an email account you've already connected in Settings
3. Warmup starts automatically. The daily scheduler runs in the background and sends warmup emails within your configured send window.

## Monitoring

The Warmup dashboard shows:

- Total sent and received warmup emails
- Replies sent (engagement metric)
- Current day of the warmup ramp
- Last run time

## Pausing and resuming

You can pause warmup at any time from the dashboard. Your warmup progress is preserved — resume when ready.
