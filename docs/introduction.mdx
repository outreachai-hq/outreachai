# Welcome to OutreachAI

**OutreachAI** is a free, open-source alternative to Lemlist and Mailchimp. It combines cold email sequences, LinkedIn automation, marketing broadcasts, and email warmup — all in one self-hosted platform.

## Why OutreachAI?

Commercial tools charge $60–$150/seat/month for features that small teams and indie founders need. OutreachAI gives you everything for free by letting you bring your own infrastructure:

- **Cold email sequences** — automated multi-step follow-up, personalized with AI
- **LinkedIn outreach** — connection requests, messages, and visit automation via browser extension
- **Marketing broadcasts** — newsletter-style emails with unsubscribe compliance and A/B testing
- **Email warmup** — gradually ramp sending volume to protect your deliverability
- **Contact enrichment** — find and verify emails using Apollo or Hunter (BYOK)
- **Team workspaces** — collaborate across campaigns with role-based access

## Open source and self-hosted

Deploy on your own server in under 5 minutes using Docker Compose. Your data stays on your infrastructure.
